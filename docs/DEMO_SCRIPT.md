# 6-Minute Demo

1. Open Ardhanarishwar and explain the three-sided platform: Skilled Candidate / Unskilled
   Candidate / Business — one engine, three tailored experiences.
2. Candidate mode → Skilled track: ask for an AI Developer career roadmap; show gap analysis
   style answer.
3. Candidate mode → Unskilled track: ask "I want a job but have no skills, what should I do?"
   and show the step-by-step, foundational-training style answer — contrast it with the
   Skilled answer to show real tailoring, not just a label change.
4. Resume Analyzer: paste sample resume and show detected skills/gaps.
5. Interview Lab: generate mock interview questions.
6. Switch to Business mode and ask a real business problem, e.g. "my sales are decreasing,
   how can I improve my online business?" — show the AI-first answer.
7. Ask a deliberately out-of-scope question (e.g. "what's the weather on Mars?") to
   demonstrate the **Human Escalation Desk**: the AI declines to guess and queues it for a
   human expert with a 24-hour commitment. Open Admin & Escalations tab to show the queued
   ticket with question, mode, candidate type and AI confidence score.
8. Show Knowledge Base/RAG and explain local retrieval — including the new Unskilled-track
   and business-escalation-model entries.
9. Explain: "I did not simply connect a paid AI API. The design is local/open-source-first,
   and when the AI genuinely doesn't know, it escalates to a human instead of guessing."
10. Show production roadmap: local Whisper/Piper, Qdrant, agents, PostgreSQL, Redis,
    Kubernetes, and a distributed human expert desk for global 24-hour coverage.
11. Close with the global vision: serving both skilled and unskilled candidates worldwide,
    plus AI-first business problem solving with human backup — and the business value this
    creates for Recruweb.
