RECRUWEB KNOWLEDGE BASE — DEMO

CANDIDATE SERVICES — SKILLED TRACK
Career planning: assess current skills, target role, gaps and learning priorities.
Resume improvement: identify weak achievements, keyword gaps and unclear project impact.
Interview preparation: generate role-specific questions and evaluate answers.
Skill development: map a target role to skills and a practical learning sequence.
Education and training: recommend structured learning based on current level and target career.

CANDIDATE SERVICES — UNSKILLED / FIRST-TIME JOB SEEKER TRACK
Foundational assessment: understand literacy, numeracy and any existing informal experience before recommending a path.
Step-by-step job readiness: break a target role into small foundational skills taught in order, not all at once.
Vocational and practical training: recommend hands-on, practice-based learning over theory-heavy courses.
Confidence building: pair each learning step with a small real task the candidate can complete and show as proof of skill.
Entry-point matching: identify apprenticeships, entry-level roles or on-the-job training programs suited to a beginner.
Local-language and low-literacy support: explain concepts in simple language and avoid assuming prior technical vocabulary.

BUSINESS SERVICES
Recruitment support: convert a job requirement into a structured role profile, skills and screening criteria.
Hiring support: create interview questions, evaluation rubrics and structured candidate feedback.
Workforce planning: identify skill demand, role priorities and training needs.
Candidate management: organize screening notes and structured feedback.
Business assistant: answer approved company knowledge questions.
Real-world business problem solving: a business owner (e.g. a manufacturer or retailer) can describe a problem such as
declining sales, difficulty finding B2B clients, or scaling online presence, and receive an AI-generated first response.

AI-FIRST, HUMAN-BACKED ESCALATION MODEL
The platform always attempts an AI-generated answer first using retrieval from verified knowledge.
If the AI's retrieval confidence is low or the question falls outside verified knowledge, the query is escalated
to a human expert team rather than the AI guessing or fabricating an answer.
Escalated queries are queued with the original question, the mode (Candidate/Business), the candidate type
(Skilled/Unskilled) and the AI's confidence score, and are targeted for human response within 24 hours.
This creates a Human + AI support model instead of an AI that pretends to know everything.

GLOBAL VISION
The platform is designed to eventually serve both skilled and unskilled individuals globally: skilled users get
gap analysis and better career/business opportunities, while unskilled users get personalized, practical,
step-by-step training toward job readiness. Businesses of any size can get AI-first problem resolution with
human expert support when required.

RESPONSIBLE AI
AI should support human decision makers and must not make autonomous employment decisions.
Production systems should use encryption, RBAC, audit logs, retention policies and tenant isolation.
